# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Everyone taking part in the project's issues, discussions, pull requests and
other spaces is expected to follow it.

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or level of experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what's best for the project and its
  users.
- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing other people's private
  information are not acceptable.
- Never ask anyone for their secret key, recovery phrase or password, and never share scam links.
  Such messages are removed and their authors blocked.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through the
contact details on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove content and warn, suspend or ban participants who break the code of conduct,
as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
